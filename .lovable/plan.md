

# The Luminary Rise — Website Build Plan

## Brand Design System
- **Colors**: Dominion Blue (#0A1D4E), Crownlight Gold (#D4AF37), Sky Blue (#3B82F6), Light Blue Mist (#E9F2FF), Warm Beige (#FCF3E3), Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- **Gradient**: Radiant Gradient — Dominion Blue → Sky Blue at 135°
- **Typography**: Montserrat (headings, bold/semibold), Inter (body text, captions)
- **Design tone**: Clean, structured, premium, accessible — with visuals evoking light, upward motion, and clarity

---

## Page 1: Homepage (Single-page with sections)

### Hero Section
- Full-width hero with the Radiant Gradient background (deep blue to sky blue)
- Large headline: **"Illuminate. Transform. Rise."**
- Sub-headline and body copy as provided
- Two CTA buttons: **"Begin Your Transformation"** (gold accent) and **"Explore EVOLVE"** (outlined/white)
- Subtle animated light/starburst motif for visual energy

### About Section
- Headline: **"More Than a School. An Ecosystem for Strategic Transformation."**
- Full about copy with the founding story and Dr Samuel Omenka mention
- Vision, Mission, and Brand Promise displayed in a clean 3-column card layout
- Core Values displayed as icon badges: Intentionality, Innovation, Integrity, Excellence, Mentorship, Community, Legacy

### Philosophy Section
- Headline: **"We Believe Growth Precedes Transformation"**
- Philosophy copy presented with clean typography
- Visual accent — a divider or subtle gradient stripe in brand colors
- Emphasis on the "whole person" pillars (Mental, Spiritual, Emotional, Strategic, Relational, Financial) shown as interconnected visual elements

### Programmes Section
- Headline: **"Structured Learning. Strategic Frameworks. Lasting Transformation."**
- Featured programme card for **EVOLVE Growth Mentorship Programme**
  - 4-month immersive experience overview
  - Four core modules listed with icons
  - "Who It's For" audience description
  - CTA: **"Apply to EVOLVE"**

### Value Proposition Banner
- Full-width accent section with Crownlight Gold highlights
- Value proposition statement prominently displayed
- CTA: **"Schedule a Discovery Call"**

### Testimonials / Social Proof Section
- Placeholder section for future testimonials
- Clean card-based layout ready for quotes and photos

### Footer
- Brand name and tagline: "Illuminate. Transform. Rise."
- Navigation links
- Social media icon placeholders
- Newsletter signup with CTA: **"Join the EVOLVE Community"**
- Copyright notice

---

## Key UX Details
- Fully responsive — mobile-first design
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- Sticky header with brand name/logo text and nav links (About, Philosophy, Programmes, Contact)
- Warm, directive tone throughout — matching the "Guide and Transformer" brand archetype
- Subtle scroll animations for section reveals
- Google Fonts: Montserrat + Inter loaded for brand consistency
